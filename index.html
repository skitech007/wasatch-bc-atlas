      Sources
      • Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium encoding). In the US this is USGS 3DEP. Slope, aspect and route stats are all calculated from these tiles in your browser.
      • Basemaps: USGS The National Map
      • Avalanche paths: Utah SGID Avalanche Paths, drawn for the 1989 Salt Lake County Wasatch Canyons Master Plan. Saved from the State of Utah UPLAN copy on 2026-10-12. Many paths have no name.
      • Forecast zones: UGRC – UAC Forecast Zones
      • Route ratings: Utah Avalanche Center route ratings
      • Elevation bands: UAC Salt Lake advisories (<8,000′, 8,000–9,500′, >9,500′)
      • Alta snow: Open-Meteo models and ensembles, corrected against the Atwater SNOTEL; see the forecast page for the method.
