      Sources
      • Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS 3DEP in the US). Slope, aspect and route stats are calculated from these.
      • Terrain class and runout reach: a simplified run of the AutoATES v2.0 method (Toft et al., 2024, NHESS 24:1779) with its default thresholds: start zones 28–60° under 50% tree canopy, energy-line runout at 18°, 24° and 33° alpha angles, then the AutoATES forest table. Tree canopy from USFS/NLCD Tree Canopy Cover 2025. Not field-checked for the Wasatch: treat it as a guide, not a verdict.
      • Low-angle shading: under 25° (the range Cody Townsend skis on high-danger days) and outside even the big-event (18°) runout reach. Only drawn where the terrain model covers (Millcreek to Alta).
      • Basemaps: USGS The National Map
      • Avalanche paths: Utah SGID Avalanche Paths (1989), State of Utah UPLAN copy, saved 2026-10-12.
      • Streams: USGS National Hydrography Dataset, used to find creek-bed terrain traps.
      • Forecast zones: UGRC – UAC Forecast Zones
      • Danger ratings: Utah Avalanche Center, via the avalanche.org public API.
      • Route ratings: Utah Avalanche Center route ratings
      • Place names: USGS GNIS
