How these numbers are made

  1. Water. Hourly precipitation from four weather models for Alta's forecast point (40.5884, −111.6386, 9,895 ft), via Open-Meteo.
  2. Alta correction. The models smooth the Wasatch into blobs and miss how much falls at Alta. For the winters of (Nov 15 – Apr 15) we compared what each model forecast 1 to 7 days ahead with what fell at the Atwater SNOTEL, and multiply each model's water by its factor for that lead day (days 8–15 use day 7):
    Model1 day out234567
    GFS jumps at day 5 and GEM at day 3, where each model switches to a coarser grid. Models with smaller day-to-day error after correction get more weight in the blend (hover a factor for details). Rerun tools/calibrate_alta.py to refresh.
  3. Rain or snow. From the temperature at 9,895 ft: all snow at 32°F or colder, all rain at 37.4°F or warmer, a straight line between (Jennings et al. 2018).
  4. Snow ratio. Kuchera method: 12:1 at about 29°F, heavier above, lighter below, from the warmer of the 9,895 ft and 700 mb temperatures.
  5. Days 8–15. The corrections are only measured out to 7 days, and a single model run that far out is mostly noise, so from day 8 the snow and water are the average of all 82 ensemble runs rather than the four-model blend. The model table still shows each model's own run.
  6. Odds and ranges. The same steps run on all 82 runs of the ECMWF (51) and GFS (31) ensembles, each corrected with its parent model's factor (the GFS ensemble keeps one grid throughout, so its factor is held at the day-4 value). "Likely range" takes how spread out the middle half of those runs is and centres it on the blend; chance figures are the share of runs that hit the mark.
  7. Everything else (temperature, feels like, humidity, wind, cloud, freezing level) is the weighted blend of the four models. Snow level comes from the NWS forecast grid and runs out after about 7 days.

Sources: Open-Meteo forecast, ensemble and previous-runs APIs (NOAA, ECMWF, Environment Canada models) · NWS api.weather.gov (snow level, forecast snow, alerts, forecast discussion) · NRCS SNOTEL 1308 Atwater. Nothing comes from OpenSnow or the resort.
